The Quiet Signal - Season 1, Episode 2: "Carrier Wave"

Mira: The relay tower has been dead for six days. Somebody is still transmitting on it.
Osgood: Dead towers do not transmit, Mira. Storms do.
Mira: Storms do not repeat the same seven tones every forty minutes.
Osgood: Then we climb it. Bring the analyser and do not touch the grounding strap.
Mira: I climbed Halloway Ridge in worse weather than this.
Osgood: Halloway Ridge did not have four hundred metres of wet lattice steel.
Mira: The signal is stronger up here. It is not coming from the antenna. It is coming from beneath the tower.
Osgood: Beneath it? There is nothing under that tower but the old Calder substation.
Mira: The Calder substation was decommissioned before either of us was born.
Osgood: Which is exactly why nobody has checked it. Get down. We are going underground.
Mira: Wait. Osgood, the tones changed. There are eight now.
Osgood: Eight tones. That is a station identifier, not a fault. Someone is announcing themselves.
Mira: Announcing themselves to whom?
Osgood: That is the part that worries me. Log everything and tell nobody at the depot until we know.
