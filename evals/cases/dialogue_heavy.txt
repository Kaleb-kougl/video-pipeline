Kettle Street Kitchen - Season 2, Episode 4: "Service"

Ines: Table nine sent back the consomme again.
Bardo: Again? That is the third time tonight.
Ines: They say it is cloudy.
Bardo: It is not cloudy, it is unfiltered. There is a difference.
Ines: Bardo, they do not care about the difference.
Bardo: Then they should eat at a place that does not care either.
Perrin: He is not wrong.
Ines: Perrin, stay out of this.
Perrin: I have been staying out of it for two years. Look where it got the restaurant.
Bardo: What is that supposed to mean?
Perrin: It means we have eleven covers on a Friday and the landlord has stopped answering my calls.
Ines: Eleven?
Perrin: Eleven. I counted twice because I did not believe it either.
Bardo: Then we change the menu.
Perrin: You have said that four times.
Bardo: And you have never let me finish the sentence. We change it to one dish. One dish, one price, no choices.
Ines: Nobody will come for one dish.
Bardo: They came for one dish in Tarsell.
Perrin: Tarsell was nineteen years ago and you were not paying the rent.
Ines: Both of you, the pass is backing up.
Bardo: Fire two consomme.
Ines: Bardo.
Bardo: Fire two consomme, Ines. Unfiltered. If table nine sends it back a fourth time, I will carry it out there myself and explain it to them.
Perrin: He will, you know.
Ines: I know. That is what frightens me.
Perrin: Give him the week. If it is still eleven covers next Friday, I will call the landlord back and we will talk about what closing looks like.
Ines: Do not say that word in the kitchen.
Perrin: It is already in the kitchen. I am only the one saying it out loud.
