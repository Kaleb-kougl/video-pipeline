Starlight Cartographers - Season 1, Episode 9: "The Ninth Chart"

Narrator: The survey ship Corolla had been mapping the Verge for two hundred days when the ninth chart came back wrong.
Captain Idris: Wrong how, Sabine?
Sabine: The parallax says the Threnody Cluster is where it has always been. The occultation timings say it moved four light-days closer while we were looking at it.
Captain Idris: Instruments?
Sabine: I ran it on both arrays and by hand. It is not the instruments.
Halden: Then it is the cluster. Things move. That is what things do.
Sabine: Not four light-days in a hundred and ten days, Halden. Not without leaving a wake.
Halden: So find the wake.
Captain Idris: Vesper, can the drive hold a survey burn out to the Threnody edge?
Vesper: The drive can. The crew is another question. We are seventy days past rotation and Halden has not slept properly since the sixth chart.
Halden: I sleep.
Vesper: You sit in the observation ring with your eyes shut. That is not sleeping.
Captain Idris: Noted, and overruled. Sabine, plot the burn.
Sabine: Captain, if the cluster genuinely moved, the eight charts behind it are all wrong too. Every world we filed is in the wrong place.
Captain Idris: Then we file a correction and we eat the embarrassment.
Vesper: It is not embarrassment. Three colony convoys are already flying on charts one through eight.
Halden: ...How long until the first one makes orbit?
Sabine: Nineteen days.
Captain Idris: Vesper, wake the transmitter. Sabine, I want the correction filed before we burn, not after.
Sabine: And if the correction is wrong as well?
Captain Idris: Then we will have been wrong faster, and that is the only advantage we have. Halden, go and actually sleep. I need you clear when we reach the edge.
Halden: I will sleep when the ninth chart makes sense.
Narrator: It would be forty-one days before the ninth chart made sense, and by then the Corolla would not be the only ship in the Verge asking the question.
