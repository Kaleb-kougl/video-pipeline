Neon Lantern Brigade - Season 1, Episode 3: "The Tram That Never Arrives"

[COLD OPEN - Kesst Terminal, 04:41]

MIRA HALVEN: Third night running. The 04:52 tram pulls in, the doors open, and nobody gets off.
BRIGADIER OKONNE: Nobody gets off because nobody got on. Look at the manifest.
MIRA HALVEN: Forty-one names. Forty-one paid fares. And an empty car.
BRIGADIER OKONNE: Then the Brigade has a case, not a rumour. Wake the lantern.

[ACT ONE - Brigade House]

TEO VASK: I ran the fare records against the district census. Every one of those forty-one names belongs to somebody who moved out of Kesst before the line was even built.
MIRA HALVEN: Dead names buying tickets.
TEO VASK: Dead names buying tickets on a loop that only exists between 04:41 and 04:52.
BRIGADIER OKONNE: Eleven minutes. That is the whole window.
MIRA HALVEN: Then we ride it.
BRIGADIER OKONNE: Mira, the last two officers who rode it filed no report.
MIRA HALVEN: They filed no report because they came back and found nothing worth writing down. I intend to bring back something worth writing down.

[ACT TWO - Aboard the 04:52]

TEO VASK: Lantern is lit. Reading holds steady at forty-one.
MIRA HALVEN: Forty-one what? There is nobody here.
TEO VASK: The lantern does not count people. It counts intentions. Forty-one somebodies intend to be on this tram.
MIRA HALVEN: Teo, the windows.
TEO VASK: I see it. The city outside is Kesst from nine years ago. Before the flood wall.
MIRA HALVEN: The tram is not going somewhere. It is going to a when.
BRIGADIER OKONNE: [over radio] Mira, your signal is arriving eleven minutes before you send it. Whatever you are going to do, I have already heard you decide it.

[ACT THREE - The Turn]

MIRA HALVEN: If the loop feeds on intention, we starve it. Teo, cut the lantern.
TEO VASK: Cutting the lantern strands us inside the window.
MIRA HALVEN: It strands the window inside us. Cut it.
TEO VASK: Lantern out. Count dropping. Thirty. Twelve. Four.
MIRA HALVEN: One.
TEO VASK: Zero. Mira, the doors.

[TAG - Kesst Terminal, 04:52]

BRIGADIER OKONNE: The 04:52 pulled in on time and forty-one people walked off it, blinking, nine years late for work.
MIRA HALVEN: Forty-one names off the manifest and onto the census.
BRIGADIER OKONNE: File it. All of it. Including the part where you decided before I heard you decide it.
MIRA HALVEN: That part goes in the appendix, sir.

[END OF EPISODE]
